Ziri Browser – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

In short: Ziri Browser has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any of your data. What you browse, save and type stays on your phone.

1. What Ziri stores on your device

These are kept only on your device, inside Ziri's private app storage, and are never sent to the developer:

  • Browsing history and bookmarks (private and Tor tabs keep no history).
  • Saved logins. If you choose to save a password, it is encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore (secure hardware where your phone has it). Filling, showing or exporting a password asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
  • Cookies, site data and cache created by the websites you visit.
  • Settings, site permission choices, downloads and video playback positions.

Ziri turns off Android's app backup, so this data is not copied to cloud backups. You can delete browsing data in Settings, remove saved logins in Settings → Passwords, or erase everything by uninstalling the app.

2. Connections Ziri makes

Ziri only contacts the following. None of these is run by the developer.

  • Websites you open. They receive a normal web request, including your IP address, and they set their own cookies. Ziri blocks many ads and trackers, removes tracking parameters from links, and sends the "Do Not Track" and "Global Privacy Control" signals.
  • Your search engine. When you search, your query goes to the engine you chose (Google by default; you can switch to Bing, DuckDuckGo, Brave Search or Ecosia). Suggestions while you type are worked out on your device from your bookmarks and history; typed text is not sent anywhere until you search.
  • Filter lists. To keep ad and tracker blocking current, Ziri downloads public block lists (EasyList and EasyPrivacy) from easylist.to. That server can see your IP address and that a download happened.
  • Site icons and titles. When you add a site as a shortcut or bookmark, Ziri fetches that site's icon and title directly from the site. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Reader view images. Pictures in an article are loaded from the article's own site, after passing the same ad and tracker filters.
  • Downloads you start. Files are fetched from the address you chose.
  • The Tor network, if you open a Tor tab. Traffic from that tab is routed through Tor relays operated by volunteers. See torproject.org for how Tor works and its limits.

3. Permissions

  • Camera, microphone, location: used only when a website asks and you allow it. The access goes to that website, not to the developer. You can change or revoke your choice at any time.
  • Photos, videos and audio (media access): used so the built-in player and file manager can list and play files on your device.
  • Notifications: to show download progress and background playback controls.
  • Biometrics / screen lock: used only to unlock your saved passwords. Android handles this; Ziri never sees your fingerprint or face data.
  • Network access: to load web pages and the items listed in section 2.

4. Sharing

The developer does not receive your data, so there is nothing to sell or share. The only parties that see your information are those you choose to connect to: the websites you visit, your search engine, easylist.to for list updates, and the Tor network if you use it. Each has its own privacy policy. Ziri includes no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries.

5. Security

Ziri prefers secure connections (HTTPS) and warns you before opening an insecure page or one with a certificate problem. Saved passwords are encrypted on your device. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and a site you visit can still collect what you give it.

6. Children

Ziri is a general-purpose browser and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
